# Short-video scripts (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

12 scripts, 15 to 45 seconds each. Post the same cut to all three apps on the same day (calendar in `PLAN.md`).

## Production notes (apply to every video)

- Format: vertical 1080 x 1920, 30 fps. Captions burned in (most people watch muted). Keep text inside the middle 80% of the frame so app buttons do not cover it.
- Screen recordings: record the live site at https://trail-hmc.up.railway.app in a desktop browser at 1280 px wide, then crop and zoom in the editor so text is readable on a phone. Run through each flow on the live site before filming and adjust shots to what the page actually shows.
- Pages used: `/demo` (replay, patterns, homework time, "Your pace", AI-use declaration), `/verify` (verify an exported record, and the tamper test).
- Face: top third of the frame, screen recording in the bottom two thirds, or full-screen recording with a small face bubble.
- First frame is the hook. No logo intro. No music with lyrics under the voiceover.
- Bio link: `trail-hmc.up.railway.app/?src=tiktok` (or `?src=reels`, `?src=shorts`). Pin a comment with the same link where the app allows it.
- Standard CTA line (spoken or on screen): "Free during early access. Link in bio to join the queue. Every friend who joins moves you up 5 places."
- Words we never use: "detects AI", "AI detector", "proves you didn't use AI", "guaranteed", anything about grades.
- If anyone other than the founder posts these for money or free access, they add "#ad" or "paid partnership" (and use the app's paid-partnership label).

## 1. "I got flagged for AI. Here's my replay."

**Only film this with a student this actually happened to, who wants to tell it.** If you cannot find one, use the alternative hook at the end. Never stage a fake accusation.

- Length: 35-45 s
- Record: `/demo` replay, or the student's own replay if they choose to share it
- Hook (0-2 s): Student to camera, flat: "My essay got flagged for AI." On screen: **"Flagged for AI. Here's what I did."**

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 s | Face, close | Flagged for AI. Here's what I did. |
| 2-8 s | Face | A detector gave a percentage. No explanation. |
| 8-20 s | Screen: replay playing at 4x, the essay building up sentence by sentence | This is my essay being written. Over 4 days. |
| 20-28 s | Screen: the sessions list or timeline showing days | Every session. Every paste and where it came from. |
| 28-36 s | Screen: `/verify` showing a verified record | Anyone can check it. No account. |
| 36-45 s | Face | Link in bio. Free during early access. |

Voiceover:
"My essay got flagged for AI. The detector gave a percentage and nothing else. What I had was this: a replay of me writing it, over four days, every session, every paste and the site it came from. It's signed, so I can't have edited it after. My professor opened the link and checked it in the browser. Trail doesn't decide whether you used AI. It just shows how you wrote. Link in bio, it's free right now."

(Only keep "my professor opened the link" if it happened. Otherwise cut that sentence.)

CTA: "Link in bio. Join the queue; every friend moves you up 5."

Alternative hook if no real case: "If I ever get flagged for AI, this is what I'll show." Same shots, voiceover in the conditional.

CTA: "Link in bio."

## 9. Professor reaction

**Film only with a real instructor who agrees, has seen the script, and is not paid.** Do not show their institution's logo or imply that their school uses or endorses Trail. Get written consent to post.

- Length: 35-45 s
- Record: `/demo` replay and `/verify`, shown on a laptop the professor is watching
- Hook (0-2 s): Professor's face watching the screen, eyebrows up. On screen: **"Showing a professor a replay of an essay being written"**

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 s | Professor reacting | Showing a professor how an essay was written |
| 2-15 s | Over-the-shoulder: replay at 4x | (their first comment, captioned) |
| 15-25 s | Paste list with sources | (their question, captioned) |
| 25-35 s | `/verify` | (their reaction to verification) |
| 35-45 s | Founder to camera | It doesn't detect AI. It shows the writing. Link in bio. |

Questions the founder asks on camera (do not script their answers):
1. "What do you look at now when you're unsure whether a student wrote something?"
2. "Is this more or less useful than a detector score? Why?"
3. "What would you want to see that isn't here?"
4. "Would you rather a student send you this, or not?"

Keep critical answers in the cut. A professor saying "I'd still want to talk to the student" is a good line.

CTA: "Link in bio. It's for students, and it's free in early access."
