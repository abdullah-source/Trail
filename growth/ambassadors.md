# Campus ambassador program

Five paid student ambassadors, one per campus, for the last two weeks of the 21-day window. Their job: get students at their school to join the Trail waitlist and confirm their email, honestly.

---

## 1. The deal

| | |
|---|---|
| Term | Day 8 (Fri 23 Oct) to Day 21 (Thu 5 Nov 2026) |
| Base pay | **$50**, paid when the week-1 checklist below is done |
| Bonus | **$25** when 100 people have joined through your link and confirmed their email |
| Max per ambassador | $75 |
| What counts | Confirmed waitlist emails that came through your `/?ref=CODE` link or your QR code. Unconfirmed sign-ups do not count |
| Perks | Early access, a direct line to the founder, a reference letter on request if you did the work |
| Payment | Venmo or Zelle within 3 days of the milestone. Abdullah keeps a record of every payment |

**Week-1 checklist (earns the $50):**
1. Onboarding call done.
2. 20 flyers up in approved places.
3. One personal post on your own Instagram, TikTok or similar, with the disclosure line.
4. Link shared in at least 3 group chats you are actually in.
5. One tabling session or one club/class announcement (with permission from the club or instructor).

**Rules:**
- Disclose every time. Any social post about Trail includes "I'm a paid Trail campus ambassador" or #ad, and uses the app's paid-partnership label where there is one. In person, say it if asked.
- No buying sign-ups: no paying friends, no raffles for sign-ups, no gift cards.
- No fake or throwaway emails. They don't confirm and they get removed.
- Follow your campus rules on flyers, tabling and solicitation. If you need a student org to sponsor a table, tell Abdullah before booking.
- Never message instructors, staff or offices on Trail's behalf. That's Abdullah's job (Trail is for students; it is not pitched to schools).
- Either side can end the arrangement at any time; base pay already earned is still paid.

## 5. What ambassadors must never say

| Never say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "It detects AI" / "It's an AI detector" / "It checks if you used ChatGPT" | "It records how you wrote. It doesn't judge it." |

| "It doesn't record anything" | "Your text stays on your laptop; the server never sees it." |

## 7. Tabling script

**Setup:** laptop open on `/demo` with the replay ready at 4x; second tab on `/verify` with an exported record and a tampered copy ready; flyers; a printed QR big enough to scan from a metre away; a sign: "Got flagged for AI? Ask me."

**Opener (5 seconds):** "Have you or a friend ever been accused of using AI on an essay?"

- If yes: "Can I show you what we built for exactly that? 30 seconds."
- If no: "Want to see what your essay looks like being written at 4x speed?"

**Demo (30 seconds):**
1. Press play on the replay: "This is an essay being written, every keystroke, sped up."
2. Point at the paste list: "Every paste, and the site it came from."
3. Switch to `/verify`: "The record is signed every 10 minutes. Here it verifies. Here someone changed one character, and it fails."
4. "It doesn't detect AI and doesn't score you. It's your record and you choose who sees it."

**Ask:** "It's free during early access. Scan this to join the queue, it takes 20 seconds. Confirm your email or it doesn't count."

**Share on the spot:** "On the page after you join there's your link. Send it to one friend right now and you move up 5 places."

**Common questions:**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Does it tell if I used AI?" | "No. It never detects AI. It records how you wrote." |
| "Can it read my essays?" | "Your text stays on your laptop. The server never sees it." |
| "Will my professor see it?" | "Only if you send them your record. You decide." |
| "Is the school making us use this?" | "No. It's for students only. It isn't sold to schools." |
| "What does it cost?" | "Free during early access." |
| "Does it work on Google Docs / Canvas?" | "It's a Chrome extension. Check the demo page for what's supported, or I'll ask the founder." |
| "What if I use AI for brainstorming?" | "It records time on AI sites as a fact. What's allowed is up to your class. It drafts an AI-use statement from your record that you edit." |
| Anything you're unsure of | "I don't know. I'll ask the founder and message you." Write their question down. |

**After each session:** note the number of conversations, scans and any question you couldn't answer. Send it to Abdullah the same day.
