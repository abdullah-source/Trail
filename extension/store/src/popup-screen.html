Before Trail records anything

Trail keeps a private record of how you write, so you can replay it, see your patterns and prove where your work came from. When you start it, it records:

  • what you type, delete and paste in Google Docs, Notion, Word Online and your school's LMS (Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, Brightspace), with timing, and the site a paste came from;
  • which sites are in front while a document is open, including AI sites (site name and minutes only);
  • time on your LMS pages, by course and assignment.

It stays on this computer. What leaves: a fingerprint of each record every 10 minutes, only if you are signed in. Never your text.
