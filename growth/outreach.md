## 3. Instructor explainer paragraph

Use in emails to staff, give to students to paste when they share a record, and put in the Product Hunt and press materials.

> **About Trail records (for instructors).** A student has shared a Trail record with you. Trail is a Chrome extension the student installed on their own laptop. While they wrote, it recorded keystrokes with timing, every paste and the website it came from, writing sessions across days, and time spent on AI sites, as plain facts. Every 10 minutes it signed a fingerprint of the record into a public append-only log, so the record cannot be changed afterwards without verification failing. You can check it at trail-hmc.up.railway.app/verify in your browser, with no account. You can watch a replay of the writing and see where each paste came from. Trail does not detect AI and does not produce a score or a judgement; what you conclude is up to you. Trail's server never sees the text. Trail is a student tool; your institution has not adopted or endorsed it.
